I'm a Product Manager at Auditoria.AI, where I own AP Invoices, our flagship invoice automation platform where AI agents read, validate, and post invoices for enterprise finance teams. As the first PM hired into the India team, I led the 0-to-1 delivery of Governed Autonomy, an agentic framework that lets finance teams run agents on their own policies and guardrails, and the accuracy work that took invoices processed with zero manual edits from under 10% to over 80%. The self-service features I shipped have also driven customer retention, upsells, and new revenue.

Previously at Jukshio, I scaled an identity verification platform to 900M+ identities verified across India, took DFraud, an AI fraud detection product, from concept to market, and added products like Video KYC and the Alternate Identity Data Platform to the suite.

Across close to 6 years of work experience in AI-native fintech, it all comes down to one question: when can a machine decide without a human watching? I answer it by making AI explainable, so enterprises can see why it decided and trust it. I stay close to the build, prototyping with AI-assisted coding and iterating with customers on real problems.

Outside of work, I find solace in books, movies, and TV shows, keep up an active lifestyle with weights, walking, and hiking, and never miss a chance to follow Formula 1 and cricket.
